🎬 Animate Project (Indah Production)

An interactive multimedia production application built with Adobe Animate and ActionScript/HTML5 Canvas, delivering a branded interactive hub for Indah Production featuring video trailer playback, showreel montages, and creative assets.

System Challenge & Solution

Elevating Multimedia Portfolios with Interactive Motion

⚠️ The Challenge

Multimedia production studios struggle to showcase diverse audiovisual works (trailers, radio commercial jingles, vector posters, and branding showreels) in a unified interactive format. Static websites fail to deliver the visceral impact and tactile engagement that video and audio demand.

✅ The Indah Production Solution

Crafted an interactive Adobe Animate application with custom frame state scripting, bespoke video player controls (pause/play, timeline scrub), radio audio visualizer triggers, and smooth animated brand reveals in a cohesive lilac and midnight slate aesthetic.

✦ Capabilities

Core System Architecture & Features

Engineered for smooth multimedia sequencing, interactive storytelling, and responsive video delivery.

🎞️

Integrated Video Player

Embedded custom-skinned video playback interface with synchronized play/pause timeline controls and cinema aspect framing.

🎨

Indah Brand Identity

Dynamic animated calligraphy mark combining stylized pencil and monogram typography with particle sparkle transitions.

📻

Radio & Audio Player

Audio playback channel streaming broadcast-quality voiceovers, commercial radio clips, and sound effect compositions.

📱

Cinematic Storytelling

Dramatic visual narrative presentation exploring social themes ("dasar punca" / "it's all your fault") through realistic smartphone prop staging.

⚡

Interactive Frame Controls

ActionScript / JavaScript frame-based navigation ensuring instant seamless transitions between About Us, Poster, Radio, Trailer, and Montage.

🏢

Corporate Vision Hub

Clear institutional presentation outlining Indah Production's mission and founding studio base in Tapah, Perak, Malaysia.
