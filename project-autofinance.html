System Challenge & Solution

Demystifying Car Loan Financing in Malaysia

⚠️ The Challenge

Purchasing a vehicle in Malaysia involves complex Hire Purchase (HP) calculations with varying fixed interest rates across financial institutions. Consumers often encounter hidden compounding costs, confusing loan terms, and lack a single unified tool to directly compare bank offers before signing dealership agreements.

✅ The Calculator Solution

Created a dynamic web calculator that takes vehicle price, down payment percentage, and tenure years to immediately project side-by-side monthly commitments, total interest accrued, and total repayment sums across major Malaysian commercial banks with 100% clarity.
