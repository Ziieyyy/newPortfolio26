Hospitality Challenge & Solution

Bridging Rural Agrotourism with Modern Digital Booking

⚠️ The Challenge

Eco-resorts and rural village homestays often suffer from low direct conversion rates due to fragmented social media posts, outdated photo sets, hidden rate cards, and confusing booking processes that require multiple manual back-and-forth messages before confirmation.

✅ The Dangau Sala Solution

A lightning-fast, visually captivating Next.js web application showcasing curated room sanctuaries with transparent rates, 1-click WhatsApp instant booking with pre-filled room templates, an engaging kampung heritage story, and interactive Mount Jerai travel guides.

✦ Capabilities

Core System Features & Architecture

Engineered for frictionless booking, immersive visual storytelling, and seamless mobile responsiveness.

🏡

Sanctuaries & Room Roster

Interactive room showcase displaying capacities (Adults/Children), bed layouts, night pricing (RM150 - RM250), and direct reservation actions for Jerai View, Pool View, Paddy View, and Malay House.

💬

1-Click WhatsApp Booking

Frictionless reservation trigger with pre-populated text templates sending guest room selections directly to resort owner En. Hadi and staff via WhatsApp.

🌴

Kampung Heritage Storytelling

Engaging narrative section honoring traditional Malay village simplicity, traditional games (Congkak, Carrom), community tourism, and 12,000+ happy visitors.

🗺️

Interactive Yan & Jerai Map

Location routing directing guests along Jalan Jemerlang Kuala Sala, highlighting landmarks including Mount Jerai, Seri Perigi Waterfall, and Titi Hayun.

⚡

Next.js Edge Performance

Built with Next.js App Router, responsive next/image optimization, sub-second First Contentful Paint, and deployment on Vercel Edge network.

📱

Mobile-First Touch UX

Floating WhatsApp quick-action bubble, smooth horizontal room sliders, responsive glass header, and touch-optimized layout for on-the-go travelers.
