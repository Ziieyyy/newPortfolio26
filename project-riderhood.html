System Challenge & Solution

Modernizing Motorcycle Service Logistics

⚠️ The Challenge

Motorcycle riders frequently face uncertainty finding reputable workshops for specialized care (such as superbike dyno tuning, major service, or genuine parts replacement). Physical queues lead to wasted hours, paper service booklets are easily misplaced, and workshop mechanics struggle with unpredictable customer walk-ins.

✅ The Riderhood Solution

Riderhood integrates an interactive GPS directory map featuring certified partner workshops (including flagship partner Wan Legacy Motor), a 5-step rapid booking scheduler with real-time bay availability, digital maintenance history receipts, and a garage inventory management hub.

✦ Capabilities

🇲🇾 Politeknik Malaysia Project 🏍️ Final Year Innovation

RIDERHOOD: Ride Smart, Service Easy!

"One App, Every Ride. Complete Peace of Mind."

Riderhood is an all-in-one motorcycle maintenance and assistance application engineered to keep riders and their bikes in top condition, anytime, anywhere. It directly tackles forgotten maintenance schedules, lack of digital service history, and difficulty finding reliable local workshops.

⚡ 6-Step Engineering Methodology
1. Problem Identification → 2. Requirements Gathering → 3. UI/UX Design → 4. Prototype Dev → 5. System Testing → 6. Evaluation
✦ 5 Core Innovation Modules
📑 Maintenance Log
Record servicing, component replacements, costs, and verified workshop logs.
⏰ Smart Reminders
Automated alerts based on mileage thresholds and calendar intervals.
⛽ Fuel & Expense
Log fuel consumption and overall motorcycle running expenditure.
🩺 Health Score
Algorithmic motorcycle health meter providing real-time condition grading.
🗺️ Nearby Workshop Finder
Discover nearby certified mechanics based on location and specific repair needs.
