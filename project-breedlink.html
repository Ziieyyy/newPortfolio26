System Challenge & Solution

Bridging Ethical Breeding with Modern UI

⚠️ The Challenge

In Malaysia and Southeast Asia, cat enthusiasts and responsible pet guardians heavily rely on unmoderated social media groups to find breeding partners. This leads to inbreeding risks, lack of verified vaccination/health records, scam transactions, and unethical breeding mills without medical certifications.

✅ The BreedLink Solution

BreedLink delivers a secure, swipe-based mobile matchmaking ecosystem featuring verified vet health certificates, pedigree lineage tracking, geolocation discovery for nearby certified breeders, encrypted in-app messaging, and mutual approval agreements to safeguard feline welfare.
